Read this before you plan lunch.

“Menu source located” means a link or menu-photo listing was found—not that its full contents were transcribed. “Prices extracted” means a published website row was machine-parsed, not that a local checkout price or today’s availability was confirmed. Some menus include drinks, sides, breakfast or multiple size variants; extracted rows are not all complete lunches.

PDF extraction candidates are kept separate until visual review. Missing prices, nutrition, allergens and walking times are never filled in. No reviews or third-party photos are reproduced here. This research atlas does not order, reserve or take payments, and does not expose the local planner’s API or personal keys.
